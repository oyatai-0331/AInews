AI news and LLM research tracker for practical daily briefing. The AI steps read
this file on every run, so keep the bullets concrete and decision-oriented.

- Major AI model, platform, and open-weight model releases from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Microsoft, xAI, Mistral, and leading open-source projects.
- Lab-focused LLM research: efficient fine-tuning, adaptation, inference, RAG, tool use, long-context systems, scientific question answering, and evaluation.
- AI agent, developer tooling, business, regulation, safety, and adoption news around generative AI, with emphasis on what changed and who is affected.
- AI agent, developer tooling, and automation updates that affect builders: Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, LangChain, LlamaIndex, Hugging Face, Vercel AI SDK, and related infrastructure.
- Business, regulation, safety, and adoption news around generative AI, with emphasis on what changed, who is affected, and what to watch next.
- Efficient fine-tuning, adaptation, and inference for LLMs, especially methods that work with limited GPU budget.
- Retrieval-augmented generation, tool use, long-context systems, and scientific question answering with LLMs.
- Evaluation methods for reasoning, agent behavior, robustness, hallucination, benchmark contamination, and honest reproducibility.
- Open-weight model releases, datasets, training recipes, safety evaluations, and papers with enough detail to reproduce or compare.

## Notes / context (optional)

- Prefer Japanese output for quick reading.
- Prioritize primary sources such as official announcements, release notes, papers, standards, and regulatory documents before commentary.
- For each item, explain the practical implication in one short paragraph: why it matters, who should care, and what action or follow-up is worth considering.
- For research items, include the core method, claimed result, evaluation setup, likely limitation, and whether the result looks actionable for a small lab or applied team.
- Avoid repeating generic "AI is growing" stories. Focus on concrete releases, policy changes, benchmarks, pricing, API/platform changes, security incidents, and enterprise adoption signals.
